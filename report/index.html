Privacy, in plain words
  • We ask for an age group, a made-up callsign, which quest you played, your device type and your answers. Nothing else.
  • To keep out spam and bots, the server limits how many reports one connection can send. It keeps your connection address only as a scrambled code that can't be turned back into the address and is deleted within 2 days. We also use Cloudflare Turnstile, a privacy-friendly bot check.
  • We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
  • Reports never ask for names, emails, photos or locations. Please don't type them in answers.
  • Adults (18+) can choose to join the crew list after sending a report. We keep the email, and a first name if you give one, separately from your report, and use it only for Forkline and Songtan Labs updates. It isn't sold or shared. We email you once to confirm; if you don't confirm within 7 days, the address is deleted. Once confirmed, it's kept until you unsubscribe or ask to be removed: email crew@playforkline.com. Under-18s never see this option.
  • Under 14, a parent or guardian must say OK first.
  • Reports are stored in Seoul (Supabase) and kept for up to 12 months, then deleted. They aren't sold or shared, and they're never used for ads.
  • The server may log your network address for a short time to keep the service running. We don't store it with your report.
  • Callsigns only appear in the credits if you say yes and your feedback is used. We check callsigns before listing them.
  • Forkline is made to be an E for Everyone game that teaches science, money and business through play.
