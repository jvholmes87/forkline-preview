Forkline: a free space trading game

A free, turn-based space trading game for phone and PC. You're a freight probe that starts with a loan. Buy where goods are made, sell where they're wanted, and pay off the debt before the interest grows.

Play free in your browser Help us test

No download, no signup. Built to an E for Everyone standard by Songtan Labs.

How it plays

One mission takes about 20 minutes. Every day you spend flying adds interest, so plan your route.

🛒

Buy where it's made

Every stop makes some goods cheaply. Water from Ceres, metals from Psyche, food from Mars.

🚀

Fly the route

Ten real places: Earth orbit, the Moon, Mars, the asteroids and three nearby stars. Trips cost days, not fuel.

💰

Sell where it's wanted

Prices jump during shortages and crash in gluts. Read the rumors, beat the market, clear your debt.

  • Market events with rumors and shortages
  • Freight and passenger contracts
  • A Daily Mission, same for everyone
  • Light-lag: star prices arrive days old
  • When a mission ends, you fork: a copy of you boots up
  • Learn supply and demand, interest and planning

What's new

Every build is tested with seeded balance bots and a phone-size check before it ships. Most changes come straight from tester reports.

v25.8Oct 30, 2026

Stars worth the trip, a fair Daily and a calm view

  • Star colonies pay more for the goods they import, and the farther the star, the more they pay. They also sell more exoplanet samples for the trip home.
  • The Daily Mission now counts your first run of the day. Play it again as practice as often as you like; practice runs don't change your counted score.
  • Share your Daily result with no routes or prices, so it won't spoil the day for anyone.
  • Calm view in the ☰ menu: bigger text, nothing moving, fewer numbers, and a buy or sell sign on every good.
  • Local records restart with this build, because star pay changes scores.
v25.7Oct 29, 2026

Sound

  • Short sound effects for trades, jumps, arrivals, events and the end of a mission, made in code so the game stays small.
  • Turn sound on or off and set the volume in the ☰ menu. Everything a sound tells you is also on screen.
v25.6Oct 29, 2026

The first five minutes

  • A guided first trade walks new players through buying, flying and selling.
  • Tap any game word (fork, colony, freight, debt…) to see what it means.
  • Banks, insurance, the warehouse and forks now unlock after act 1, one at a time.
  • The Move screen shows where your contracts and passengers are going, with a bigger "Show all" button.
  • Tester reports no longer need a code, and this home page is new.

A new home and an icon

  • Forkline moved to playforkline.com and got its "F" icon for browser tabs and phone home screens.
  • Adult testers can join the crew list for updates, with an email to confirm.

Tester reports and credits

  • A tester report you can send from the game menu or the end screen, with questions for each age group.

Tips for new players

  • Short tips explain each idea the first time it matters, with a setting to turn them down or off.

Markets come alive

  • Every stop buys and sells every good; wider margins between where goods are made and where they're wanted.
  • Market events: rumors, then shortages or gluts that spread, worsen or fade.

  • Forks you build count toward your score.

Story seasons

After launch, Forkline's story grows one chapter a month, three chapters a season, each tied to real space science happening that month. These are working titles: plans can change, and testers help shape them.

Season 1 · working title

The KESTREL Question

Tallis Freight's rival probe keeps undercutting your routes. Who programmed it, and why does it know where you're going?

Season 2 · working title

Inheritance

A fork comes back changed. What does a copy owe its original?

Season 3 · working title

Colony Councils

A colony runs short, and the councils vote on who supplies them.

Season 4 · working title

Open Lanes

The Solar Transit Authority opens shared trade lanes, and the solar system starts to feel a lot less empty.

We plan to keep finished chapters playable, so nobody misses the story.

How we build Forkline

A small studio, building in public, with players instead of around them.

👂

Players first

We read every tester report, and the changes it leads to are listed on this page.

📖

Depth, not grind

A real story, real science and a monthly chapter, so there's always a reason to come back.

⚖️

Fair play

The full mission is free. The Daily Mission is the same for everyone. No pop-up ads.

🧪

Tested before it ships

Every build runs through seeded balance bots and phone checks, and big updates go to testers first.

🔒

Privacy by default

Tester reports ask for no names or emails, and the site uses no tracking cookies.

🛰️

Learn by playing

Supply and demand, interest, planning and light-speed delay, built to an E for Everyone standard.
