About Forkline

Forkline is a free, turn-based space trading game for phone and PC, with no download and no signup. You run a freight probe across ten stops from the Moon to Tau Ceti: buy goods where they are made, sell them where they are wanted, and pay off your loan before the interest grows.

Shortages, gluts, freight and passenger contracts, and light-lag prices from the stars keep every mission different. Built to an E for Everyone standard. Updates and news · A Songtan Labs game.
